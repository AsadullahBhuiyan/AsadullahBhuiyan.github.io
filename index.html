Asadullah
Bhuiyan

Hi there! I’m Asad, a PhD candidate in theoretical physics at Cornell working on high-dimensional stochastic systems in quantum many-body physics and machine learning. I combine analytical theory and Python simulations to understand how complex behavior emerges from randomness, interactions, and learning.

I’m especially interested in problems whose underlying structure isn’t obvious: finding the right effective description, building simulations to test it, and turning complex behavior into a clear mechanism. This has drawn me increasingly toward AI interpretability, alignment, and safety—questions I expect to become more urgent in the coming years. These problems remain difficult to formulate precisely, and I believe a physicist’s toolkit can help sharpen the relevant questions and make sense of the complexity involved.

I value clear reasoning, collaboration, intellectual honesty, and empathy, and I’m always open to conversations across research, technology, and decision-making.
